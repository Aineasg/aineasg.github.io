NET.HARDWARE-REPAIR

Hardware Repair

Self-taught, and the skill I have the most bench time in. Component-level work on laptops and desktops — I diagnose to the failing part before reaching for a replacement board, and I document the fix. The examples below are things I have actually done.

🔌
A repair I've done
BIOS Chip Replacement & Recovery

Desoldered a laptop's SPI BIOS chip, reprogrammed it on an external programmer, and resoldered it — first attempt, no board damage — to recover a machine that would not POST.

Hover for detail

What it involved
  • Identifying the SPI flash package and pinout
  • Clean desolder and resolder of an 8-pin chip
  • Read / write / verify on an external programmer
  • Sourcing a correct firmware image and reflashing
  • Confirming a full boot afterwards

Homelab: a networking problem I worked through

Ran an LLM in a QEMU VM with all its traffic forced through a VPN with a kill-switch. The VPN's TUN interface and QEMU's virtual bridge conflicted; I worked it out with manual iptables rules and a separate network namespace. Slow going, but it taught me more about Linux routing than any lab.
